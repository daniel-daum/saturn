Tracking the changes to this website

v0.7.0 — 2026-10-13 — new coat of paint

a pixel font, five colour themes, and a theme button

The site is set in Geist Pixel now, and its colours come from Flexoki. Each visit gets one of five palettes (moss, ink, wine, plum and teal), and the theme button in the footer moves to the next one. Posts no longer sit in a panel, and the saturn screenshot changes to match.

The themes need one small script, the only one on the site. It keeps the name of your last palette in your browser and nothing else, and the privacy page says so. Without JavaScript, the site stays moss.

v0.6.0 — 2026-10-13 — static again

no more micro posts, likes, or comments

Micro posts, likes, comments by email, and the hourly sync behind them are gone. The site is plain HTML and CSS again, with nothing running on a server, and the privacy page is shorter for it.

v0.5.0 — 2026-10-08 — comments and a cleaner look

likes, comments by email, and a calmer post layout

Likes and comments by email are back, rebuilt to keep less about you. The privacy page says exactly what's stored. Micro posts are gone, the garden trackers moved to their own pages at /books/, /movies/, /shows/, /games/ and /music/, and links to the site now show a preview card.

Posts sit in a panel on a darker page, with one quiet footer for likes and comments, previous/next cards underneath, and easier-to-read headings and text, especially on phones.
