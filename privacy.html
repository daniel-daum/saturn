THE PRIVACY POLICY | 1 SEPTEMBER 2026

There is no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no ads. I don't know who visits this site.

IF YOU LEAVE A LIKE:

Liking a post sends one form submission. I store a count per page and a daily-rotating hash of your connection so you can't like twice in a day. I can't get your IP back out of it, and it's gone in two days.

IF YOU LEAVE A COMMENT:

Replies are public: your display name, your message, and the time are shown on the post. Your email address is never stored. I keep a keyed hash of it for rate limiting only. Mail to the reply address passes through Cloudflare.

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, so Cloudflare sees your IP address and the page you asked for. I haven't enabled request logging, so I never see it. Cloudflare's privacy policy is here.

If you email me, I have your email. That's it.
