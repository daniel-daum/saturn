about | how its made

I have never been completely satisfied with the look of my websites. In my efforts to improve what I don't like, I end up redesigning it - over, and over, and over. That being said, simple, minimal sites have always appealed to me. Doing less somehow feels both calming and satisfying, and this version is my closest attempt so far.

The implementation is deliberately plain: static HTML and CSS, no client-side JavaScript or framework, registered and hosted on Cloudflare. I write HTML & CSS locally on my machine, and when changes hit main my self-hosted gitea runners push the changes to cloudflare pages.

As of september 2026, I think I've finally become happy with the overall look and feel of the site, enough to call it 'complete', at least stylistically. :)
