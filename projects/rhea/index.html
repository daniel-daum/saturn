about | how its made

Rhea is a Mac app that turns grocery receipts into data. You drop in a stack of receipt PDFs, and a vision model pulls out the store, the total and every line item. Then you check each one next to the original scan, confirm it, and export the lot to CSV or Postgres. The idea was to track what groceries cost across stores over time.

Everything runs locally. The extraction goes to whatever model you point it at (LM Studio, oMLX, anything that speaks the OpenAI chat API), so no receipt ever leaves your machine. It's plain SwiftUI with no third-party dependencies.

It was also an experiment. I designed and wrote it almost entirely through conversation with Claude, from the architecture down to the bug fixes, to see how far that could go on a real project.

Rhea is archived. The code works, but it's rough. It's a case study, not a product.
