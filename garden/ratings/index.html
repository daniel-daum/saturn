/RATINGS

How I score things in the garden. One number out of five.

THE SCALE

Everything in the garden gets one score out of five. The score is about how much I enjoyed it, not how well made it is.

rating label what it means
(1/5) terrible Didn't finish it, or wish I hadn't.
(2/5) below average Finished it, but wouldn't recommend it.
(3/5) average Fine. No regrets, no strong feelings.
(4/5) above average Really liked it. Would recommend.
(5/5) great A favorite. It goes on the favorites shelf at the top of its page.

Half points are allowed. Anything I haven't scored yet shows as (?/5).

THE COLORS

Each score has its own color so a wall of covers is easy to scan. Red for the low end, green for the high end.

--rating-terrible

#e86b6b

--rating-below-average

#d9a441

--rating-good

#bdbd7f

--rating-above-average

#a9c94f

--rating-great

#7ec98f

The number is always shown next to the color. In high contrast mode the colors get brighter.

IN MARKUP

A rating is one element with two classes. The modifier sets the color, and the link goes back to this page. 

<a class="rating rating--great" href="/garden/ratings">(5/5)</a>

Modifiers, 1 to 5: rating--terrible, rating--below-average, rating--good, rating--above-average, rating--great. Leave the modifier off for an unrated entry.

On a tracker tile the caption is already a link, so the rating there is a span instead of a second link. It looks the same. 

<span class="rating rating--great">(5/5)</span>
