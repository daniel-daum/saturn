NO SCRIPTS, JUST FORMS

This site now has likes and replies, and there is still not a single line of JavaScript on it. The heart below is a plain HTML form. Pressing it sends one request, the server counts it, and you land right back here. You can like a post once a day; nothing about you is stored to enforce that, only a hash that stops matching tomorrow.

Replies arrive by email. The link at the bottom opens your mail client with the subject already filled in. Send the message, and it appears on the post a moment later with your display name and the time. Your address is never stored and never shown. To answer someone, use the reply link under their comment and the thread nests itself.
