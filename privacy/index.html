Privacy Policy

15 October 2026

Trackers and analytics

There are no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no ads. I don't know who visits this site.

The one thing kept on your device is the name of the colour theme you last saw, in your browser's storage, so your next visit can get a different one. It isn't a cookie, it never leaves your browser, and clearing this site's data resets it.

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, so Cloudflare sees your IP address and the page you asked for. I haven't enabled request logging, so I never see your IP. Cloudflare's privacy policy is here.

Likes

If you like a post, I store a count for that page, and a scrambled code made from your IP, browser, and the date, so you can't like twice in a day. It can't be turned back into your IP, and it's deleted after two days.

Comments

Comments come in by email. Your address is only used to check if it's me, then it's gone. I keep your name, your comment, and the time, and all of it shows on the post. Cloudflare's email log keeps the sender for a bit. Email me if you want your comment taken down.

Email

If you email me, I have your email. That's it.
