the privacy policy | 8 October 2026

There are no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no ads. I don't know who visits this site.

If you like a post, I store a count for that page, and a scrambled code made from your IP, browser, and the date, so you can't like twice in a day. It can't be turned back into your IP, and it's deleted after two days.

If you comment, you send it to me by email. Your address is checked once, to see if the comment is from me, and then forgotten. It is never saved, hashed, or logged. What's saved is the name from your "name:" line, your comment, the post, and the time, and all of that is shown on the post. Quoted replies and signatures are cut off first. I get an email with your name and comment, not your address, so I can delete it, or approve it if it has a link. If you want a comment taken down, email me.

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, so Cloudflare sees your IP address and the page you asked for, and comment emails arrive through Cloudflare too. I haven't enabled request logging, so I never see your IP. Cloudflare's privacy policy is here.

If you email me, I have your email. That's it.
