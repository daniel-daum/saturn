WAKE ME UP WHEN SEPTEMBER ENDS|3 SEPTEMBER 2026

This page highlights what I've been doing recently. It updates on occasion, so check back every now and then, if you'd like. It is September 2026, and it has been 8 months since my first update in January.

RECENTLY

College

This year, I finished the spring and summer semesters at Laney, a community college in Oakland, and I’m starting the fall semester. I’m taking Discrete Mathematics, Data Structures and Algorithms, and a history class—nothing too exciting. What’s exciting is that next spring will be my final semester at community college before I transfer! I’m currently applying to transfer to a four-year university. My hope is to transfer to UC Santa Cruz for a B.S. in Computer Science. It’s also internship application season, so I’m hoping to pick up a summer software engineering internship next summer (if you know of any, please reach out!).

Coffee

I recently started getting into coffee. I’ve been making espresso at home and got addicted. I didn’t understand the coffee cult until recently, but drinking a warm espresso while reading the morning news on the balcony brings me so much joy each morning. I found a local coffee shop nearby called Mother Tongue. They have great beans, and it’s women-owned!

Running

I decided, for some reason, that I wanted to run a half marathon, so I’ve started training for it. yay.

Gaming

I’ve had less time for gaming lately. I recently finished the 2D platformer REPLACED (3/5). It’s absolutely gorgeous and perfect for the Steam Deck. I was originally planning to play Alan Wake 2, but I’ve decided to wait for Spooky Season to really dive into it! If I have time I hope to also finally get to the Dead Space Remake.

Reading

I wish I had more positive notes here. I recently finished Hard-Boiled Wonderland and the End of the World (2/5) by Haruki Murakami, Carmilla (2/5) by Sheridan Le Fanu, and A Court of Mist and Fury (2/5) by Sarah J. Maas. Nothing great to report here, so on to the next one -> Dungeon Crawler Carl

Listening

WOR$T GIRL IN AMERICA (5/5) (Pop) from Slayyyter is amazing and perfect. It feels like this album was made specifically for me, and I’m so excited to see her live in concert soon!

I’m also enjoying Navy Blue (4/5) (alternative) by Porch Light, who will be releasing a new album, Hush, later this month.

Watching

If you’re into comedy or horror, I can’t recommend Widows Bay (5/5) enough. It’s fantastic.

This site

I finally decided to put more effort into this site, so expect to see more frequent updates and changes in the near future! Come back soon
