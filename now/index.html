Wake Me Up When September Ends

3 September 2026

This page highlights what I've been doing recently. It updates on occasion, so check back every now and then, if you'd like. It is September 2026, and it has been 8 months since my first update in January.

College

This year, I finished the spring and summer semesters at Laney, a community college in Oakland, and I’m starting the fall semester. I’m taking Discrete Mathematics, Data Structures and Algorithms, and a history class—nothing too exciting.

What’s exciting is that next spring will be my final semester at community college before I transfer! I’m currently applying to transfer to a four-year university. My hope is to transfer to UC Santa Cruz for a B.S. in Computer Science. It’s also internship application season, so I’m hoping to pick up a summer software engineering internship next summer (if you know of any, please reach out!).

Life

I recently started getting into coffee. I’ve been making espresso at home and got addicted. I didn’t understand the coffee cult until recently, but drinking a warm espresso while reading the morning news on the balcony brings me so much joy each morning. I found a local coffee shop nearby called Mother Tongue. They have great beans, and it’s women-owned!

I also decided, for some reason, that I wanted to run a half marathon, so I’ve started training for it. yay.

Media

I’ve had less time for gaming lately, and I wish I had more positive notes on reading, but the music has been great.

Playing

  • REPLACED (3/5): a 2D platformer that’s absolutely gorgeous and perfect for the Steam Deck
  • Alan Wake 2: waiting for Spooky Season to really dive into it
  • the Dead Space Remake, if I have time

Reading

  • Hard-Boiled Wonderland and the End of the World (2/5) by Haruki Murakami
  • Carmilla (2/5) by Sheridan Le Fanu
  • A Court of Mist and Fury (2/5) by Sarah J. Maas
  • on to the next one -> Dungeon Crawler Carl

Listening

  • WOR$T GIRL IN AMERICA (5/5) (pop) from Slayyyter: amazing and perfect. It feels like this album was made specifically for me, and I’m so excited to see her live in concert soon!
  • Navy Blue (4/5) (alternative) by Porch Light, who will be releasing a new album, Hush, later this month

Watching

  • Widows Bay (5/5): if you’re into comedy or horror, I can’t recommend it enough. It’s fantastic.

I finally decided to put more effort into this site, so expect to see more frequent updates and changes in the near future! Come back soon
