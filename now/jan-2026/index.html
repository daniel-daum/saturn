My First Now Page

21 January 2026

This page highlights what I've been doing recently. It updates on occasion, so check back every now and then, if you'd like. This is the january 2026 edition. The most current update can be found here.

Christie and I just wrapped up our second year as Californians. We've been exploring California, trying to make new friends, and just settle into our new lives

california pros

  • consistent weather
  • access to national parks
  • redwoods

california cons

  • no snow, or fall :(
  • old friends are far away

Anyways, it's a new year and I'm trying to ride out that 'this year will be different' feeling for as long as possible. Just the usual stuff: more exercise, more reading, and maybe ease off some of the bad habits a little

Reading

Recently I was recommended "The Will of the Many" by James Islington, and I immediately devoured it. If you enjoy reading fantasy, this should be on your list

I was able to pick up a copy of book two, "The Strength of the Few", which released recently. It is also great, and every second I have to spend not reading it is pure agony. Highly recommend

Listening

The familiar

  • miguel ft j.cole - all i want is you (r&b)
